Join the beta

How it works

Code calculates your chart, and AI only explains it, so nothing is guessed.

  1. Add your birth details. Date, time and place of birth.
  2. Code calculates your chart. Every planet, house and timing period is worked out by fixed calculations.
  3. AI explains it in plain words. It reads those facts and answers your question. It never makes up a chart fact.
